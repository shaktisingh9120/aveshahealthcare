About us

Revenue cycle partners with a care-first mindset

For more than 18 years we've helped providers get paid accurately and on time, so clinicians can stay focused on patients.

Company overview

Specialists in the business side of healthcare

Medora began in 2008 as a small coding team serving anesthesia groups. Today we support more than 35 specialties with 2,500 people across delivery centers in the US and India.

Our model is simple: pair specialty-trained people with technology that removes repetitive work, then measure everything. Each client gets a dedicated account team, agreed KPIs and complete visibility into their revenue cycle.
